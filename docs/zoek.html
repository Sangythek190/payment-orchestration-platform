Product Decisions
Why Wix Payments?
Zoek's 10,000+ SMBs already use Wix for their e-commerce storefronts. Wix Payments is the native gateway embedded in that ecosystem. Building on the existing payment rail eliminates onboarding friction and reduces time-to-value to under 7 days.
Why OAuth 2.0?
API key rotation at scale (10,000+ accounts) is operationally expensive. OAuth 2.0 with refresh tokens provides automatic credential management, scoped permissions, and a standard revocation flow. It also aligns with Wix's own authentication model.
Why 3 Actions, Not 1?
The payment lifecycle has three distinct states: immediate charge (point of sale), deferred billing (invoice sent), and payment collection (invoice paid). Collapsing these into one action forces partners to build workarounds. Three granular actions cover 95% of e-commerce payment workflows.
Why Zero Custom Code?
BYOPG was designed as a reusable automation architecture from day one. The same three actions work across Wix, PayFast, and Stripe with only configuration changes. This cuts onboarding time for new partners from weeks to days and eliminates per-partner maintenance debt.
