Product Manager Work
What I Did
The PM thinking behind this infrastructure.

Problem Identification

Identified that every partner using a third-party payment system had the same 6-step manual bottleneck. The pattern repeated across Partner A, Partner B, and Partner C — each with different payment gateways but the same activation delay.

Requirements Design

Designed the 3-action automation architecture (Charge Order, Send Invoice, Pay Invoice) that works with ANY webhook-capable payment gateway. Wrote acceptance criteria covering happy path, partial failures, duplicate webhooks, and retry logic.

Reusability Principle

Insisted on reusable infrastructure over partner-specific solutions. The first implementation (Partner A) took engineering effort. The second (Partner B) and third (Partner C) plug into the same 3 actions with configuration only — no new code.

Market Validation

Benchmarked against Amplitude 2025 data: customers reaching first value within 14 days retain at 80%+. BYOPG compressed time-to-first-value from days to minutes — closing the activation gap that drives early churn in SaaS (70% of churn happens in first 90 days).

Cross-Partner Scaling

Validated with 3 partners across 3 continents (US, South Africa, Italy) — each with different payment systems, currencies, tax requirements, and billing models. The architecture handles all of them without per-partner code.

Platform Impact

BYOPG doesn't just serve payment partners. The same webhook-to-activation pattern powers AI product deployment — when a partner's customer pays, their AI Voice Receptionist, AI Chat, or Reputation AI activates automatically. Payment infrastructure becomes AI distribution infrastructure.
