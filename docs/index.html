Product Management Work

What it took to design and deliver this infrastructure

🔍
Problem Identification
Mapped the 6-step manual payment onboarding process, identified bottlenecks through partner support ticket analysis, and quantified the cost of each manual handoff.
📝
Requirements Design
Authored the Mission Brief with evidence-based objectives, exhaustive acceptance criteria (GIVEN/WHEN/THEN), and explicit failure signals for every success metric.
🔄
Reusability Principle
Designed 3 automation steps that work identically across all payment gateways — no per-partner custom code, no per-partner maintenance burden.
📊
Market Benchmarking
Benchmarked against Stripe (first API call in 30 min) and Twilio (self-service onboarding) to set measurable outcome targets for activation speed and partner onboarding efficiency.
🌍
Cross-Partner Scaling
Coordinated across 3 partners in 3 countries (US, South Africa, Italy) with different payment ecosystems, currencies, and compliance requirements.
⚡
Platform Impact
Designed infrastructure that addresses 650K+ businesses, reduces per-activation cost to near-zero, and creates a repeatable pattern for every future partner integration.
